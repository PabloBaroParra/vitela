# Vitela privacy policy

_Last updated: 5 October 2026_

This policy covers **Vitela for Windows**, distributed through the Microsoft
Store.

## Short version

Vitela works entirely on your computer. It does not collect or transmit
personal data, and it sends nothing to its developer or to any third party.
It contains no analytics, advertising, telemetry, crash reporting or account
system, and it makes no network connections.

## What the app reads

- **The documents you open.** They are read and edited only on your device,
  and they are written only where you choose to save them.
- **Your signing certificate**, only when you use **Sign**. You choose where
  it comes from: a certificate in your Windows user store, a `.pfx`/`.p12`
  file you pick, or a smart card or token through the driver you select. The
  certificate is used to sign the document on your device. Vitela never
  stores it, never copies it elsewhere and never sends it anywhere.
- **Windows Recent items.** Vitela adds the PDFs you open to the Windows
  *Recent* list and reads that list back to show them on its Recent screen.
  That list belongs to Windows, and you can clear it in the Windows settings.

## What the app stores

- **A small diagnostic log** of recent failures, kept on your device. Its
  entries contain the error category, the operation, the time and random
  identifiers. They never contain file paths, document content or passwords.
  The log is capped at 256 KB and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Passwords and PINs you type are kept in memory only while they are needed,
for example while a protected document is open. They are never written to
disk.

## Data shared with Microsoft

Microsoft handles the download, installation and updates through the Store,
under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).
Vitela does not add anything to what the Store itself collects.

## Children

Vitela collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version changes any of the above, this page will be updated
before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
